goblin privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Who uses goblin

goblin is a private app used only by me to archive my lecture notes.

Google user data goblin accesses

goblin requests only the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, goblin creates one folder in my Google Drive and uploads, lists and pins revisions of files inside that folder. It cannot see or change any other content in the account. goblin does not request the user's profile, email, contacts or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Files are encrypted on my device before upload, so Google Drive stores only encrypted copies. The data is used solely to keep an off-device backup for the lecture notes. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or training of any model, and is not read by anyone other than me.

Sharing

goblin does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to any third party.

Limited Use

goblin's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
